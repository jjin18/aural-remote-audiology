Purely Hearing interaction references

The user supplied Uiverse.io button and striped-light examples by
uiverse-astronaut and dexter-st. The current pill interaction and striped
signal field are substantially rebuilt adaptations, with native link/button
semantics, responsive SVG, keyboard focus and a reduced-motion fallback.

An earlier button iteration used BHARGAVPATEL1244 / great-catfish-18.
Source: https://github.com/uiverse-io/galaxy/blob/adbd2adde0a299a3956ea288fb444ec01891ca41/Buttons/BHARGAVPATEL1244_great-catfish-18.html

CSS Mesh — organic morph keyframes
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Source: https://github.com/stevesouth/css-mesh
Adapted source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/stevesouth/css-mesh/main/src/lib/css-mesh/animations/morph-animation.ts
Related reference: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/stevesouth/css-mesh/main/src/lib/css-mesh/themes/base-themes.ts
License source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/stevesouth/css-mesh/main/LICENSE

The six-stage translate/scale/rotate keyframe pattern is adapted into the
site's liquid-morph CSS animation. Timing, amplitude, radial light fields,
colors and reduced-motion behavior are customized for Purely Hearing. The React
component library is not included.

Codrops — On-Scroll Typography Animations, effect6
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Source: https://github.com/codrops/OnScrollTypographyAnimations
Adapted source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codrops/OnScrollTypographyAnimations/main/src/js/index.js
Demo/article: https://tympanus.net/codrops/2023/01/18/on-scroll-typography-animations/
License source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codrops/OnScrollTypographyAnimations/main/LICENSE

The perspective, X-axis rotation, upward movement and staggered opacity
reveal of effect6 are adapted to whole words with restrained distances.
The implementation uses Motion mini and native scrolling. Codrops' GSAP,
Lenis, Splitting, fonts and image assets are not included.
